ROADPRINT EASY UPDATE

1. Close the Chrome tab showing ROADPRINT.
2. Extract this ZIP somewhere new, such as Desktop.
3. Open the ROADPRINT_iPhone_Release_Project folder.
4. Double-click START_ROADPRINT.bat.

This is a revised prototype: fixed missing icon glyphs and includes a one-click Chrome launcher. GPS and local trips are prototype features; full navigation, cloud accounts, Crews and CarPlay are NOT implemented.

Your old project is not overwritten. Trips stored in the previous Chrome session may not automatically carry over to the new launch.
